# Glossary

One entry per word this company uses with a meaning of its own, in this open-source CompanyOS repository.

| Term | What it means here |
|---|---|
| quarter close | The four working days after the last day of a quarter, when the books for that quarter are reconciled and frozen. |
| owner | The one person accountable for a supplier, a customer or a decision. There is exactly one per thing. |
| within tolerance | A difference a person has agreed to accept without dispute; the figure is in `finance/tolerance.md`. |
