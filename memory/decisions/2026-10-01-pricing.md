# 2026-10-01 — List pricing moves to a single published price

A decision on file in this open-source CompanyOS repository: what changed, when, and why.

**Decision.** The company publishes one list price per product and stops quoting per-deal prices to new customers.

**Reason.** Two customers on the same volume were quoted 18% apart in September, and the difference was found in a support thread rather than in the books.

**Replaces.** Nothing. This is the first pricing decision on file.
